Who Is Who?

Court cases can involve many different people. Select the role that best describes the person you want to learn about.

🛡️

Defendant

The person or party responding to a legal claim or accusation.

📄

Plaintiff

The person or party who generally brings a civil claim against another party.

👁️

Witness

A person who may provide information about events or facts relevant to a case.

⚖️

A judicial officer who handles matters brought before the court.

👨‍⚖️

Lawyer

A legal professional who may represent or advise a person involved in a case.

🏛️

Witness Guide

A witness may provide information about events or facts relevant to a case.

👁️

Know What You Know

Focus on what you personally saw, heard, experienced, or otherwise know.

🗣️

Give honest answers and do not intentionally change or exaggerate information.

❓

Listen Carefully

Listen to each question carefully before answering.

Follow any applicable restrictions about discussing testimony or case information.

Court Process

This is a simplified overview of steps that may occur in a court case. Actual procedures vary depending on the type of case and location.

📄

1. Filing

A case generally begins when appropriate documents are filed with the court.

📬

2. Notice

The other party may receive formal notice of the case or claim.

📝

3. Response

Depending on the case, the responding party may have an opportunity to answer.

🔎

4. Preparation

Parties may gather documents, identify evidence, and prepare their arguments.

🏛️

5. Hearing or Trial

The court may hear arguments, testimony, and evidence according to applicable rules.

⚖️

6. Decision

The court may issue a judgment, order, or another decision.

Important!!!

Court procedures can differ significantly depending on the jurisdiction and type of case. Always check official court information for the rules that apply to your situation.
