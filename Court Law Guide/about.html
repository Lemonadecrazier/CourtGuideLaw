ABOUT COURTGUIDE

Making court information easier to understand.

A simple educational resource for people trying to understand unfamiliar court processes.

OUR PURPOSE

Court language can be difficult. We want to make it clearer.

CourtGuide was designed to organize common legal terminology and court concepts into information that is easier to navigate.

Instead of presenting everything as one large block of legal language, the website breaks information into categories and interactive sections.

The goal is to help visitors understand what certain terms mean, what questions they might have, and what topics they may want to discuss with a qualified legal professional.

CourtGuide provides general educational information and does not provide legal advice.

Laws and court procedures vary by jurisdiction. Information on this website should not be used as a substitute for advice from a qualified lawyer or official court resource.
